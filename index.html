University of Technology Sydney

Sydney, Australia

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher and received my Ph.D. from the University of Technology Sydney (UTS). My research lies at the intersection of cooperative perception, federated learning, autonomous driving, and embodied AI.

I work on advancing multi-agent perception systems for autonomous driving, with a focus on Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) cooperative perception — enabling vehicles to share sensory information to achieve more robust and comprehensive scene understanding. I am also interested in federated learning for privacy-preserving distributed training, 3D Gaussian Splatting for novel view synthesis, and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models for robot manipulation.

My recent work includes NeuPAN (T-RO 2025), an end-to-end model-based learning approach for direct point robot navigation, developed in collaboration with the HKU robotics group. I have also contributed to cooperative perception with learning-based V2V communication, self-supervised adaptive weighting for cooperative perception, and road-supervised federated learning with bug-aware sensor placement.

Research Interests Cooperative Perception · Federated Learning · Autonomous Driving · V2V Communication · Robot Learning · Embodied AI · 3D Gaussian Splatting · VLA Models
