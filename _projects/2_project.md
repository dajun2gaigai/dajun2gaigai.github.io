**NeuPAN** (published in IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 2025) is an end-to-end model-based learning approach for direct point robot navigation. It integrates neural network-based perception with model-based optimization to achieve safe and efficient navigation in complex environments. The method has received 60+ citations and was featured as a Top 50 Popular Paper.
