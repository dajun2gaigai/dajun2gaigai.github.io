Exploring **3D Gaussian Splatting** (2DGS/3DGS/4DGS, LAGS, ominiRecon) for novel view synthesis and **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models** (VLA-Adapter, VLA_HiDE) for robot manipulation. This research direction aims to bridge high-fidelity 3D scene reconstruction with embodied intelligence for real-world robotic tasks, integrated with **CARLA** autopilot simulation for autonomous driving applications.
