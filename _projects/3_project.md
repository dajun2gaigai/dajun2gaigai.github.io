This project develops **federated learning** methods for privacy-preserving distributed training in autonomous driving scenarios. Key contributions include road-supervised federated learning with bug-aware sensor placement, and decentralized privacy preservation for critical connections in graphs.

**Key Publications:**
- *Road Supervised Federated Learning With Bug-Aware Sensor Placement* (IEEE TVT, 2024)
- *Decentralized Privacy Preservation for Critical Connections in Graphs* (IEEE TKDE, 2024)
- *C2P-M: Critical Connection Protection in Multiplex Graphs* (IEEE TKDE, 2026)
