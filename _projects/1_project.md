Cooperative perception enables multiple vehicles to share sensory information to achieve more robust and comprehensive scene understanding. This project explores **learning-based V2V communication** for efficient feature sharing, **self-supervised adaptive weighting** for robust perception, and **denoising techniques** for communication-robust cooperative perception.

**Key Publications:**
- *Cooperative Perception With Learning-Based V2V Communications* (IEEE WCL, 2023) — 37 citations
- *Self-Supervised Adaptive Weighting for Cooperative Perception in V2V Communications* (IEEE T-IV, 2023) — 28 citations
- *Coop-WD: Cooperative Perception with Weighting and Denoising for Robust V2V Communication* (IEEE T-ITS, 2026)
